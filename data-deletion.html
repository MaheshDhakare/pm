How to delete your data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the information PropCola Connect holds about you at any time. This covers feedback you gave at a site or showroom, details you entered in a buyer app, and — for sales team members — your name and mobile number used for the daily WhatsApp summary.

Send us a request

Email YOUR-EMAIL@propcola.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and tell us:

  • your name;
  • the project, builder or showroom you visited or work with;
  • the mobile number or email address you used, if any, so we can find your information;
  • roughly when you visited or gave feedback, if you remember.

What happens next

  • We reply within 7 days to confirm we received your request. We may ask a question to make sure the request comes from you.
  • We delete your information, or make it anonymous so it can no longer identify you, within 30 days.
  • We tell you when it is done.

Totals that no longer identify you (for example “40 visitors this month”) may remain in reports. We keep information longer only if the law requires it.

Other quick options

  • Stop notifications: turn off notifications for the buyer app in your phone or browser settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Stop the daily WhatsApp summary (sales team members): reply to us or ask your employer to remove you from the team list.
