Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

PropCola Connect (“PropCola”, “we”, “us”) is a service that real estate builders and showrooms (“our clients”) use to share project updates with their buyers, collect visitor feedback, and keep their sales teams informed. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Visitor feedback: when you fill in a feedback form (for example by scanning a QR code at a site or showroom), we store your answers, the time of your visit and, if the form asks for them, your name and mobile number.
  • Buyer app: if you install a project’s buyer app and turn on notifications, we store a device token for sending notifications and the details you enter, such as your name, unit and mobile number, plus your RSVP replies and any feedback you give in the app.
  • Our clients’ sales teams: names, roles and mobile numbers of team members, entered by the client or by us on their behalf, so that we can send each team member a daily WhatsApp summary of the visitors they attended.
  • Dashboard users: the email address and name of people who log in to manage projects.
  • Technical information: basic information needed to run the service, such as when an app was installed or last opened, and information sent by your browser.

How we use it

  • To show project updates, documents and events to buyers, and to send them notifications they have agreed to receive.
  • To give our clients’ management reports about visitor feedback, so they can improve their service and follow up with visitors.
  • To send our clients’ salespeople a daily WhatsApp summary of the visitors they attended, and the details of those visits when they ask for them.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The client you visited (the builder or showroom) and the people they authorise, such as management and the sales head. Notes you leave for the management are shown only to them, not to salespeople.
  • Service providers that host or deliver the service for us: Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (website hosting), Google Firebase (app notifications) and Meta (WhatsApp messages). They process information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as the client’s project is active with us, so that their reports stay complete. When a client stops using PropCola, or when you ask us to delete your information, we delete it or make it anonymous within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off app notifications at any time in your phone or browser settings.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information. See how to delete your data.
  • Salespeople who no longer want the daily WhatsApp summary can ask their employer or us to stop it.

Security

Management reports are protected with an access code and stored privately. Access to our dashboard is limited to authorised accounts. No method of storing information online is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit who can see it.

Children

PropCola Connect is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
